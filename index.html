Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your tattoo session.

We highly recommend booking an appointment in advance to secure your preferred artist. However, we do accept walk-ins on Saturdays depending on artist availability.

A $100 non-refundable deposit is required to lock in your session date. Deposits go directly toward the final cost of your tattoo. Rescheduling requires at least 48 hours advance notice.

Get a full night's sleep, stay hydrated, and eat a solid meal prior to arriving. Wear comfortable clothing that grants easy access to the area being tattooed.

You must be at least 18 years of age with a valid government-issued photo ID. We strict studio policy: no tattoos for minors under 18 under any circumstances.
