FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details about sizing, production timelines, and worldwide shipping.

Made-to-Order pieces are chosen from our signature catalog and tailored to your measurements. Bespoke Tailoring is a completely custom 1-of-1 design crafted from scratch for your specific event.

We provide an illustrated measurement guide that makes taking your dimensions easy with a measuring tape. You can also book a quick virtual video consultation or request our sizing chart on WhatsApp.

Ready-to-Wear Kimonos & Bubus ship within 3–7 business days. Made-to-Order pieces take 7–14 days. Bespoke couture takes 3–6 weeks. Rush orders are available upon request.
